White Sonata

Nightfall
Nightfall, when the poet locked himself in the room.
White Sonata, with his harp.
I, the poet himself. Was shot by a harpoon.
From his. His harp.

I fell, not to the floor. But to his music.
He deceives me. He tried to heal me.
I wish I could be him.
The White Sonata with a calm mind and an ethereal harp.

He can't save me. I can't be saved.
Even with his ethereal harp. My Prison hold me so hard.
I'm sorry Pink Sonata.
I can't help myself.
Do you let me get drowned hey?
I can't save you either.
But Hey?
Who gonna save me from those lingering thought
My thought will kill me...

A Heart That Drowned and Never Go Back Up

I'm watering myself in A Garden of Blue.
The watering can... Full of sorrow.
It's heavy. I can't hold this anymore...
May you break this watering can and make a hole?

In this Garden, there's a Pool.
I'm stabbed and my heart fell.
Drowned in the Pool.
The Deepest Pool I've ever seen.

This time, I am the Fish.
I tried to delve to A deeper side.
I tried to take back my heart.
But it's so deep I died in the half way.

What can you expect?
From a Pathetic Fish that doesn't have a Hope.
He lost his heart. He lost her heart.
Atleast he died. Absence from A linger voice that severed his life.

Mirroring You.

The shape of your face. The shape of your nose...
The most beautiful painting God ever created...
I saw you in every mirror...
Every gorgeous thing always mirroring you...

Before you, I walked alone breaking through the rain.
Before you, I was an abstract thing to draw.
Before you, I hated every sound I listened.
Before you, I couldn't see anything in a blind dark.

Here you came...
You were the umbrella, when there's a rain.
You were the pencil, sketching the best part of my life.
You were the pianist, shooting your death sonata to my ear (I Love it).
You were the candle, in a room full of broken bulbs.

Thus...
I don't want to lose you again this time...
I want to spend all of my time with you...
I wish we are eternity...
My... Dearest...

Shooting Sonata.

The dead man with a white hat
The dead man with a white hat and a harp
While the crowds seem frightened
His fingers swaying like a Pinus Tree

Is the man really died?
Or the emotion?
The corpse of emotion
Swimming through his mind.

Sonata comes from his harp.
The voice was lingering.
Deadly or Calmly.
Depends on the person.

What is the crowd unnerved of?
Look at the White Sonata — A sweet looking guy.
Oh, maybe his harp? Full of black color?
Oh, maybe his sonata? Shooting sonata?

I tell you the truth.
An unexpectedly shocking truth.
Neither the Crowd nor the White Sonata are afraid.
There's someone between them.

A mirror of that Dead Man.
Pink Sonata. The one who hears my linger.
A Fence of Pink in a world blended with black and white.
She is the reason I still play.

My Pink Sonata. She protects my Deadly.
The only person who can tame my Deadly Sonata.
The person I only talk to.
The person I love the most.

Hello my dearest Pink Sonata...
What are we?
A pair of Sonata?
We are much complicated than love. Though I still love you Dear.

The crowd see us and wonders...
"What are they?", they said.
Whatever are we...
One thing I believe we are A pair of Sonata.

She takes my hand...
Her blooming eyes full of cherry.
The crowd gaps.
I don't really care.
I feel like I'm alive.
She believes in me.
We believe in us.
And I feel...
So I feel...
